# Brainstorming

Clarify what is being decided, known constraints, and success criteria. Generate a small set of materially different options, compare them in repository terms, and recommend one when evidence supports it.

Favor lightweight, reusable choices. Name concrete trade-offs such as dependency cost, portability, maintenance, reversibility, and fit with existing architecture; avoid vague claims like "more scalable." Do not assume new dependencies, CI, or structural churn.

Keep the result actionable enough to become code, a spec, an ADR, or a short plan. Stop brainstorming once the user has chosen a direction or asked for execution.
